# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Households and individual cooks who want to know what food they have, use it
before it goes bad, and decide what to cook without manual inventory work.

## Product Purpose

Leftovr turns grocery receipts into a kitchen that keeps track, highlights what
to eat first, and suggests meals from food already at home. Product requirements remain
defined in `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`.

## Brand Personality

Low-effort, warm, and practical. Copy frames useful opportunities without guilt,
warnings, clinical nutrition language, or exact-expiry anxiety.

## Anti-references

Avoid calorie trackers, heavy inventory tools, alarm-driven expiry apps, generic
SaaS copy, and interfaces that make food tracking feel like administrative work.

## Design Principles

- Make receipt-to-kitchen value immediately understandable.
- Reduce cooking decisions and manual tracking.
- Use specific, plain language.
- Celebrate useful food choices without guilt.
- Preserve English and Serbian localization.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic markup, keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, reduced
motion support, and translation-safe layouts.
