Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Notice for TwoBrickLabs ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we collect, store, use, and share ("process") your personal information when you use leftovr, including our mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). We are the controller of that information.

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the Services.

Summary of Key Points

What do we process? Your account details, your household and its members, the food you add, your preferences and avoidances, recipe activity, notification settings, and the technical data needed to run the app.

Do we sell your data or use it for advertising? No. We do not sell or rent personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who else sees it? The members of your household, and the service providers listed in section 3 who run parts of the Services for us. Receipt, e-receipt and pantry photos are sent to Anthropic to be read and are deleted as soon as they have been read.

Sensitive information? Avoidances you choose to enter can reveal a health, dietary or religious restriction. We only use them to fit recipes to your household.

Your control. You can export your data and delete your account at any time in the app's Settings.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, name (from your sign-in provider, or the part of your email before the @), and a user identifier. We do not store passwords; you sign in with Apple, Google or a one-time email code.
  • Household: kitchen name, invite codes, members and their roles, and your household's currency and plan.
  • Food and activity: items you add (by receipt, e-receipt, pantry photo, barcode or by hand) with their quantities, prices, dates and storage place; what you mark as used, cooked or thrown away; recipes generated for you, favourites and cooking history.
  • Preferences: language, cooking and shopping preferences, goals, serving counts, and avoidances.
  • Photos and files: receipt, e-receipt and pantry photos and receipt PDFs you choose to scan. These are processed only to read the items and are not kept (see section 7).
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, such as support requests or feedback.
  • Website waitlist: if you join our waitlist on leftovr.co, your email address.

Sensitive information. We do not ask for health, religious, or similar data as such, but avoidances you choose to enter may reveal it. We process them only with your explicit consent, given by entering them, and only to fit recipes to your household. You can remove them at any time in the app.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data: push-notification token, time zone, app version, device type and operating system, and IP address (which our hosting providers see when your device connects).
  • Error reports: when the app hits an error, a report with the error message, technical details and the app version. Error reports do not include your name, email address or account identifier.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe, the app store tells us (through RevenueCat) which plan you have and when it renews or ends. We never receive your card or payment details.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifier.

2. Why Do We Process It, and on What Legal Basis?

  • To provide the Services (accounts, households, food tracking, scans, barcode lookup, recipes, reminders, subscriptions, data export and deletion). Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.
  • To fit recipes to avoidances. Legal basis: your explicit consent.
  • To send notifications you have switched on. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • To keep the Services secure and working, fix errors, prevent abuse and fraud, and enforce usage limits. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a safe and reliable service.
  • To understand use in aggregate, for example how many accounts are deleted and how far people got. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the Services.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting rules or lawful requests from authorities. Legal basis: legal obligation.

We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. AI is used only to read photos and to suggest recipes and images.

3. Who Do We Share It With?

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as described here.

Household members

Everything in a shared kitchen, including its food, recipes, cooking history, who added or used what, members' names, and the avoidances that shape the kitchen's recipes, is visible to its other members.

Service providers

These companies process data on our behalf, under contract, and only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage, and server functions (hosts all account and household data).
  • Anthropic: reads receipt, e-receipt and pantry photos and receipt PDFs, and writes recipe ideas. It receives the photo or file, or, for recipes, the names of items in your kitchen with the household's avoidances, serving count and chosen filters. It does not receive your name, email address or account identifier, and does not use this data to train its models.
  • OpenAI: generates recipe illustrations from a short text description of the dish. It receives no personal information.
  • Open Food Facts: an open product database. When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to look up the product.
  • RevenueCat, with Apple and Google: manage subscriptions and receipts. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and purchase records.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose them, and push-notification delivery, through Expo's notification service.
  • Sentry: error reports (no name, email or account identifier).
  • Vercel: hosts our website. Cloudflare (Turnstile) protects the waitlist form from bots, and Resend stores waitlist emails and sends them. Google Fonts serves the website's fonts, which shares your IP address with Google.

Other cases

  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order or a lawful request from authorities, or when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, your information may be transferred, and this notice will continue to apply to it.

4. Cookies, Tracking and Analytics

The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you. It stores a few settings on your device (for example, that you have seen the AI notice, and a draft of an unfinished scan) and keeps you signed in using your device's secure storage.

Our website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The waitlist form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which may process technical data about your browser to tell people from bots.

We do not respond to "Do-Not-Track" signals because we do not track you across sites in the first place.

5. Sign In With Apple or Google

If you sign in with Apple or Google, the provider tells us your email address (or an Apple private-relay address), a provider identifier, and, if you allow it, your name. We use these only to create and secure your account. The provider's own privacy policy governs what it does with your data.

6. International Transfers

We are based in Serbia. Some of our service providers are in the European Union, the United States, or other countries whose data-protection laws may differ from yours. When we transfer personal information out of Serbia, the EEA or the UK, we rely on adequacy decisions or on standard contractual clauses (or equivalent safeguards) with the provider. You can ask us for details.

7. How Long Do We Keep It?

  • Scan photos and files: deleted from our storage as soon as they have been read, normally within seconds. The items read from them stay in your kitchen.
  • Account and household data: kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account: your profile and personal data are deleted. A kitchen where you were the only member is deleted with everything in it. In a shared kitchen, the food and recipes you added stay for the remaining members, without your name attached. We keep a minimal record (a random identifier, dates, and counts such as how many items you had) to understand why people leave; it contains no name, email or content.
  • Waitlist emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Error reports and server logs: kept by our providers for a limited period, normally no more than 90 days.
  • Legal records: as long as the law requires (for example, tax records).

Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten on a rolling basis, so deleted data can remain in a backup for a short time before it disappears.

8. How Do We Keep It Safe?

We use encryption in transit, access controls so that only members of a household can read its data, private storage for scan photos, and server-side keys that never reach the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you live where a higher age applies for consenting to data processing (for example, up to 16 in parts of the EU, or 15 in Serbia), you need a parent's or guardian's consent below that age. If we learn that we have collected data from a child without the required consent, we will delete it. Contact us if you believe this has happened.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (including under Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection, the EU and UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • access your personal information and receive a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing; and
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Do it yourself in the app

In the app you can export all your data and delete your account (both in Settings), change your preferences and avoidances, turn off notifications, and leave a kitchen. Deleting the app does not delete your account; use Settings.

Or ask us

Email support@twobricklabs.com. We will answer within one month (this can be extended where the law allows) and may need to verify your identity first.

Complaints

You can complain to a data-protection authority. In Serbia, that is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs). In the EU or UK, you can contact the authority where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.
