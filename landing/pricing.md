# Pricing: leftovr

iPhone app. Prices in USD, billed through the App Store. Last updated 2026-11-10.

## Free

- Price: $0, forever
- Limits: one scan of either kind (pantry photo or receipt) ever; one recipe generation ever,
  which returns one recipe idea
- Included: unlimited barcode scanning, unlimited manual add, full kitchen with
  eat-first ranking, automatic depletion when you cook
- Note: a scan that finds nothing does not use up the free scan

## Premium

- Price: $6.99/month, or $44.99/year (≈ $3.75/month, 46% less)
- For: one cook
- Limits: 100 scans/month and 30/day; 150 recipe generations/month and 50/day
- Included: unlimited pantry-photo, receipt and e-receipt scanning; unlimited recipes ranked by what
  expires first, three ideas per generation; a daily "what to cook" suggestion; weekly and monthly insights

## Household

- Price: $9.99/month, or $69.99/year (≈ $5.83/month, 42% less)
- For: up to 3 people in one home
- Limits: same per-person scan and generation limits as Premium
- Included: everything in Premium, one shared kitchen synced across up to 3 people, shared
  recipes and insights, invite links for members

## Notes

- No ads. Receipt data is never sold.
- Cancel any time in the App Store; access runs to the end of the paid period.
- Refund policy: https://leftovr.co/refund/
- Currently in early access. Join at https://leftovr.co/#get
