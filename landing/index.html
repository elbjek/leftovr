How it works

Receipt in. Dinner out.

Three steps, one of which you already do every time you shop.

1

Snap your receipt

One photo after the grocery run, or a PDF e-receipt from your inbox. leftovr reads it, figures out the food, and skips the shampoo. Barcode scan works too for single items.

2

Your kitchen keeps track

Everything lands on shelves with gentle freshness windows — "best today", "this week" — never scary expiration countdowns.

3

Cook what you have

Recipes built from what you have, starring whatever should be eaten first. Cook one and those ingredients leave your kitchen automatically — no re-tracking.
