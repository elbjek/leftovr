What is leftovr?

The app that already knows what's in your kitchen.

leftovr is an iPhone app that turns the food you already own into dinner. Photograph your fridge or pantry — or scan a grocery receipt — and leftovr fills your kitchen, ranks everything by what should be eaten first, and generates recipes from those ingredients only.
