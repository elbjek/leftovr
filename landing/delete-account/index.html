Delete your leftovr account

Last updated: 24 November 2026

leftovr is made by TwoBrickLabs. This page explains how to delete your leftovr account and what happens to your data.

In the app

  • Open leftovr and go to the Settings tab.
  • Under Account & data, tap Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Want a copy first? Tap Export my data in the same place before you delete.

Without the app

Email support@twobricklabs.com and ask us to delete your account. Write from the email on your account, or tell us how you sign in (Apple, Google or email) so we can find it. We delete it within one month and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and personal data: name, email and settings.
  • A kitchen where you were the only member, with everything in it.
  • In a shared kitchen, the food and recipes you added stay for the remaining members, without your name attached.

To understand why people leave, we keep one short record: the account's random ID (no longer linked to your name or email), when the account was created, your plan and app language, the goal and shopping style you picked when you set up, whether you finished setup, and how many kitchens, items and recipes you had. It contains no name, email or food and recipe content. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, so deleted data can stay in a backup for a short time. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions so you aren't charged again.
